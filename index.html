Track every sale from your live show.

Backstage Live runs alongside your Whatnot show on iPad, captures each sale and giveaway as it happens, and gives you a clean PDF manifest the moment you wrap.

What it does

📡

Auto-captures every sale

Watches your Whatnot show in the in-app browser. Sales, giveaways, and raids land in the live feed instantly.

🗄

Sequential bins per buyer

Each new buyer gets the next bin number. Same buyer wins multiple items? Same bin, automatically.

📄

One-tap PDF manifests

End the show, get a print-ready PDF listing every buyer, their items, and their bin. Saved to Files for easy sharing.

📝

Per-item notes

Tap any item to add a note — shipping reminders, condition details, anything you want to remember.

➕

Add missed sales

Couldn't keep up? Tap "+ Add sale" any time to log one you missed. Bin auto-assigns from the running counter.

🔒

Local-first, private

Your sales, buyers, and notes stay on your device. No tracking, no ads, no data sold to anyone.
